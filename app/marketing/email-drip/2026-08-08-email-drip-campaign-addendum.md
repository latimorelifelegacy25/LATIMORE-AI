## Overview: Why Repeated Touches Matter

One follow-up rarely wins a client. Research consistently shows that most families need 5–7 meaningful touchpoints before they feel confident enough to take action. The Email Drip Campaign System automates those touchpoints so every lead — regardless of how they found you — receives a consistent, trust-building experience from day one.

The goal is not to pressure anyone. It's to stay present, add real value, and make it easy for people to move forward when they're ready. A lead who came in through a QR code at a PAHS event, a Facebook DM, or a Google Business Profile click all deserve the same respectful, informed follow-up sequence. This system delivers that at scale.

## Six-Stage Framework

| Stage | Name | Core Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | Confirm receipt, set expectations, establish trust |
| 2 | Education | Teach one concept that helps them understand their protection need |
| 3 | Social Proof | Build local credibility through community and client proof |
| 4 | Soft Ask | Invite a no-pressure consultation or coverage review |
| 5 | Urgency Nudge | Explain the cost of waiting — without fearmongering |
| 6 | Re-engagement | Add fresh value and reopen the conversation |

## Automation Rules (Implementation Spec)

### Numbered Rules

1. **Trigger:** New lead record created in CRM with a valid lead source tag.
2. **On entry:** Apply source tag + sequence tag + `stage:new-lead`. Send Day 0 email and text immediately. Create timeline entries for Days 2, 5, 10, 17, 30.
3. **If lead books a call:** Stop all sequence automation. Move pipeline stage to Booked. Create a pre-call prep reminder task for Jackson 1 hour before the appointment.
4. **If lead replies to any message:** Pause automation. Flag record for manual follow-up. Move pipeline stage to Engaged. Assign task to Jackson within 24 hours.
5. **If lead opts out (replies STOP or unsubscribes):** Stop all messaging immediately. Apply tag: `opted-out`. Remove from all active sequences. Do not contact again without explicit re-consent.
6. **No-response rule:** If no reply and no booking after Day 10, create a manual task for Jackson: "Call or personal DM — [First Name] — no response after 10 days." Include call script note: *"Hi [First Name], this is Jackson with Latimore Life & Legacy. I just wanted to make sure my messages were reaching you and see if you had any questions about protecting your family. No pressure at all — just want to make sure I'm helpful."*
7. **Re-engagement:** At Day 30, if no engagement (no opens, no replies, no booking): Apply tag `cold`. Optionally move to long-term nurture list (90-day re-touch). Remove from active drip.

### Pseudo-Flow Diagram

```
Lead comes in
  → Source tag applied + sequence assigned
    → Day 0 email + text sent
      → Booked? → STOP sequence | Move to Booked | Create prep task
      → Replied? → PAUSE sequence | Move to Engaged | Manual follow-up task
      → Opted out? → STOP all | Tag opted-out
      → No response by Day 10? → Manual call task for Jackson
      → No engagement by Day 30? → Tag cold | Long-term nurture or close
```

## KPIs to Track

| KPI | Target | How to Measure | Weekly Action If Below Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email open rate | 35%+ | CRM / email platform analytics | A/B test subject lines; try different send times (Tue/Thu 9 AM or 7 PM) |
| Click-through rate | 3%–8% | Link click tracking in CRM | Simplify CTA copy; use a single link per email instead of multiple |
| Reply rate | 5%+ | CRM reply tracking | Add a direct question in the email body to prompt responses |
| Booking conversion | 10%–20% | Booked stage count / total leads | Shorten booking friction; offer a phone option alongside the link |
| Sequence completion | 80%+ | % of leads who reach Day 30 without opting out | Check for deliverability issues; ensure unsubscribe link is clean |
| Cold lead revival | 5%–10% | Re-engaged leads from Cold tag | Refresh Day 30 email copy; add a new value hook quarterly |

### Weekly 10-Minute Review Checklist

- [ ] Pull open rates and CTR for emails sent this week
- [ ] Check reply rate and move all replied leads to Engaged
- [ ] Confirm all leads with no response after Day 10 have a manual task assigned
- [ ] Review pipeline stage distribution (New Lead / Nurturing / Engaged / Booked)
- [ ] Check QR scan counts against campaign baseline
- [ ] Identify any opted-out leads and confirm they are removed from all sequences
- [ ] Note one thing to test or improve next week

### A/B Tests to Run

**Test 1 — Subject Lines**
- Variant A (curiosity): "How much coverage does your family actually need?"
- Variant B (direct): "A quick protection question, {{first_name}}"
- Measure: Open rate over 30-day cohort

**Test 2 — CTA Phrasing**
- Variant A (soft): "Book a 15-minute call whenever you're ready"
- Variant B (action-oriented): "Pick a time now — it takes 60 seconds"
- Measure: Click-through rate and booking conversion rate

## Bottom Line

Every lead that enters the Latimore Life & Legacy pipeline deserves a consistent, respectful, and value-driven follow-up experience. This system delivers that automatically: the moment a lead is created and tagged by source, the drip begins. Emails and texts go out on a 30-day cadence across six stages — from welcome to re-engagement — each one designed to build trust and reduce friction. When a lead books, the automation stops and Jackson steps in. When a lead goes cold, a task is created so no one falls through the cracks. The pipeline stages in Supabase reflect reality at every step. The result: more booked conversations, fewer lost leads, and a community-centered brand experience that feels personal at scale.
