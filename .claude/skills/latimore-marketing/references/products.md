# Latimore Life & Legacy — Product Portfolio Reference

## Life Insurance Products

### ADDvantage® Term
- Level premium guarantee periods: 10, 15, 20, 30 years
- Minimum face value: $100,000 | Annual policy fee: $65
- Broad convertibility; ideal for mortgage protection and income replacement
- ADBE rider: Accelerated Death Benefit for critical, chronic, and terminal illness
- **Best for:** Young families, mortgage protection, affordable death benefit

### Protection Builder IUL® 2
- High death benefit guarantee with strong cash value build
- Optional PGR (Premium Guarantee Rider) extends No-Lapse protection to age 120
- Fidelity Multifactor Yield Index 5% ER with 1% current interest bonus
- 15-year surrender period
- **Best for:** Permanent protection with long-term guarantee focus

### Builder Plus IUL® 4
- Designed for high cash value growth and tax-advantaged retirement income
- Index interest bonuses; variable/fixed policy loans available in year 3
- 10-year surrender charge period
- **Best for:** Business executives, high-income earners, retirement supplement

### Smart Builder® IUL 3
- Early liquidity focus — most accessible IUL option
- WOSC Rider (Waiver of Surrender Charge Option): 0% premium load, 0% surrender charge if selected
- 10% penalty-free withdrawal starting in year 2
- **Best for:** Emergency reserve replacement (Smart Money strategy), early access needs

## Business & Financial Solutions

| Solution | Core Use Case | Key Prospect |
|---|---|---|
| **Annuity Maximization** | Move unneeded annuity funds into tax-free leveraged death benefit | Retirees with excess annuity assets |
| **Buy-Sell Agreements** | Protect business continuity against partner death/disability | Business owners with partners |
| **Executive Bonus (S162)** | Employer-funded IUL owned by key employee; deductible to business | Business owners wanting to retain leaders |
| **Key Person** | Mitigate financial loss of a mission-critical employee | Any business with indispensable staff |
| **Split Dollar Plans** | Split policy benefits between employer and employee to reduce tax drag | Corporate executive retention |
| **College Funding** | Grow cash values in IUL on primary earner to fund tuition tax-free | Parents with children 5–15 years from college |
| **Estate Planning** | Create liquidity at death to pay estate taxes without forced liquidation | High-net-worth families with illiquid assets |
| **Income Protection** | Safeguard dependents from loss of primary earner income | Breadwinners with dependents |
| **Legacy Building** | Leverage idle savings/RMDs/annuities into a larger tax-free legacy | Retirees wanting to pass wealth efficiently |
| **Longevity Planning** | Grow cash values to defer longevity risk past expected lifespan | Pre-retirees worried about outliving assets |
| **Pension Maximization** | Take highest single-life pension payout, use IUL to protect surviving spouse | Retirees with pension decisions |
| **Smart Money** | Transfer emergency reserves into Smart Builder IUL for growth + access via WOSC | Savers with low-yield savings accounts |
