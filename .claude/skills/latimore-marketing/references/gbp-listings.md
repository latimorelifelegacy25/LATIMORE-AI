## UTM tracking convention

When linking to any page below from a GBP post or listing field, append:
`?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp&utm_content=<product_or_service>_<slug>`

Example: `https://www.latimorelifelegacy.com/pages/term-life-insurance.html?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp&utm_content=service_term_life_insurance`

For non-GBP channels (Facebook, email, SMS), swap `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` to match the channel (e.g. `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<campaign_name>`) but keep the `utm_content` slug for consistent attribution.
